## Source keys and verification links

- **G1, G2, G3, AC, W:** the primary-paper table in [Goodwillie calculus](notes/goodwillie-calculus.md). The complete bibliography of [Calculus III](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0310481), printed pp. 710–711, supplies 19 distinct authors across its 17 entries. Its two printed Arone variants are reconciled using [the survey's public author record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.00803).
- **BW:** Ben Walter, [Rational Homotopy Calculus of Functors](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0603336); author record and abstract only in this pass.
- **Quantum texts:** the publisher and author records in [the diagram note](notes/diagram-reuse.md), including Coecke and Kissinger's book, Coecke and Duncan's paper, and Coecke, Pavlovic and Vicary's bases paper.
- **Diagram-software papers:** [homotopy.io](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13179); [PyZX attribution](https://github.com/zxcalc/pyzx#attribution); [DisCoPy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.02975v3); [Quantomatic](https://arxiv.org/html/1503.01034v2); [Catlab license](https://github.com/AlgebraicJulia/Catlab.jl/blob/main/LICENSE).
- **Formalization sources:** exact source revisions and relevant author headers, README citations, and project descriptions are linked in [Stacks and formalization](notes/stacks-and-formalization.md). The [Blakers–Massey source header](https://agda.github.io/cubical/Cubical.Homotopy.BlakersMassey.html) gives its proof lineage; the [mechanization paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.03227) identifies the earlier work.
- **Stacks h-topology reference:** [Goodwillie and Lichtenbaum bibliography entry](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/bibliography/ph).
