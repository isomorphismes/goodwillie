## How to read the evidence

**Reading notes** summarize identified parts of mathematical sources and give original explanatory examples. **Source inspection** identifies an upstream declaration, implementation, or documented feature; it is not a local build result. **Proposals** describe things we might try and do not imply that the example, interface, or mathematical formalization has been implemented in this repository.

ZX-calculus supplies useful examples of diagrams with meanings and lawful transformations. Its quantum vocabulary need not become this project's vocabulary. The notes leave room for several mathematical presentations rather than selecting a single permanent interface now.

The source-specific reading depths and links are recorded in the corresponding notes. Open questions remain attached to the concept that motivates them.
